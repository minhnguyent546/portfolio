I am an Independent AI Researcher. I work on natural language processing and deep
learning. My research interests are inference-time scaling, symbolic reasoning,
high-performance model serving, embedding models, image-text retrieval for
Vietnamese, optimal transport, retrieval-augmented generation, and image
classification.

I also build personal projects on distributed training, TPU training, and
retrieval-augmented generation for multiple-choice question answering over
complex documents.

I am familiar with concepts in algorithmic competitions. I wrote two articles for
[VNOI Magazine](https://oj.vnoi.info/magazine/): virtual trees in 2024, and techniques with the XOR operation in 2023. I was a problem setter and tester for the CICT Collegiate Programming
Contest at Can Tho University in 2025, and for the CICT selection contests for
the Vietnam Student Olympiad in Informatics in 2024 and 2025.
