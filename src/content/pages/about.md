I am an AI researcher focusing on **neuro-symbolic reasoning**,
**inference-time scaling**, **multimodal representation learning**, and
**optimal transport**. On the engineering side, I work on LLM fine-tuning,
retrieval-augmented generation, and low-latency model serving.

My papers cover a **vision-language model** for Vietnamese image-text retrieval,
transparent educational question answering, and _model soups_ for Mekong Delta
heritage image classification. Outside formal papers, I build evaluation
benchmarks, distributed and TPU training pipelines, and retrieval-augmented QA
systems over technical documents and structured data.

I also have a background in competitive programming. I competed in ICPC contests
and the Vietnam Student Olympiad in Informatics for several years. I wrote two
articles for [VNOI Magazine](https://oj.vnoi.info/magazine/), on virtual trees
(2024) and on XOR techniques (2023), and served as problem setter and tester for
Can Tho University's selection contests for the [ICPC](https://icpc.global/) and
the [Vietnam Student Olympiad in Informatics](https://www.olp.vn/).
