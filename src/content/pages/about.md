I am an AI researcher working on language models, reasoning, and deep learning.
My main focus is **neuro-symbolic reasoning**, **inference-time scaling**, and
**multimodal representation learning**. I also work on high-performance model
serving, embedding models, Vietnamese image-text retrieval, and
retrieval-augmented generation.

My papers cover a **vision-language model** for Vietnamese
image-text retrieval, explainable educational question answering, and _model
soups_ for Mekong Delta heritage image classification. On the engineering side, I have built personal projects on
distributed and TPU training and on retrieval-augmented question answering over
dense technical documents.

I also have a background in competitive programming. I competed in ICPC
contests and the Vietnam Student Olympiad in Informatics for several years. I
wrote two articles for
[VNOI Magazine](https://oj.vnoi.info/magazine/), on virtual trees (2024) and on
XOR techniques (2023). I also served as problem setter and tester for Can Tho
University's selection contests for the [ICPC](https://icpc.global/) and the
[Vietnam Student Olympiad in Informatics](https://www.olp.vn/).
