  A DSPy pipeline that generates executable reasoning programs from Vietnamese
  financial text, tables, and charts using a 4B vision-language model (GRM-2.5).
  Fine-tuning with LoRA rejection-sampling SFT raised held-out accuracy from
  **52.7% to 56.3%**, and self-consistency voting reached **67.13% program
  accuracy** on the private test.
